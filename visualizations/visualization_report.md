# Task 3: Data Visualization Report

## Objective

The objective of this task is to transform the raw quotes dataset into meaningful visualizations that make patterns and insights easier to understand.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- VS Code

## Dataset

The dataset contains 50 quotes with three columns:

- Quote
- Author
- Tags

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Top 10 Authors by Number of Quotes

The bar chart shows the authors with the highest number of quotes in the dataset.

**Key Insight:**
- Albert Einstein has the highest number of quotes with 8.
- J.K. Rowling and Marilyn Monroe have 6 quotes each.
- Dr. Seuss and Bob Marley have 3 quotes each.

### 2. Top 10 Quote Tags

The bar chart shows the most frequently occurring tags associated with the quotes.

**Key Insight:**
- `inspirational` and `love` are the most frequent tags with 9 occurrences each.
- `life` appears 8 times.
- `humor` appears 5 times.
- `books` and `reading` appear 4 times each.

## Data Story

The visualization shows that the dataset is strongly represented by a small number of popular authors. Albert Einstein is the most frequently represented author.

The tag analysis shows that inspirational, love, and life-related themes are dominant in the dataset. This indicates that the collection mainly focuses on motivational and emotional themes.

## Conclusion

Data visualization makes the patterns in the quotes dataset easier to understand. Bar charts were used to compare author contributions and tag frequencies. These visualizations convert raw textual data into clear and understandable insights.
